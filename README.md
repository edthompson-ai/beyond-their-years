# Beyond Their Years

**A practice game that hides math and language reps inside play.**

<sub>[← Ed Thompson's profile](https://github.com/edthompson-ai)</sub>

---

## Result

Live at [beyondtheiryears.org](https://beyondtheiryears.org) and [play.beyondtheiryears.org](https://play.beyondtheiryears.org).

## Problem

Kids need lots of practice, and practice is easy to skip. The goal: practice a kid would choose to do.

## What I did

Directed AI agents and Claude Code builds, under the review and approval rules in [ai-bot-operation](https://github.com/edthompson-ai/ai-bot-operation), to launch a supplemental practice game. It sits on top of whatever school or homeschool a family already uses.

**Math, science, and language games**, live on the site.

**Polyglot Island**, language practice in Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and English.

**A phone-first design check** of the marketing site at 390 px and 320 px, with fold and button fixes shipped.

## What broke and what I changed

**A removed feature still showed up in parent guides.** On Oct 24, 2026, review found guides that still pointed parents to a button that no longer existed. The release was held until the guides were cleaned up.

**A flyer draft got ahead of the site.** On Oct 30, 2026, review flagged a flyer draft that promised a feature the live site didn't have yet. The fix became a written rule: anything not built is labeled "coming soon," and the rewritten copy is in review.

**A claim got ahead of the product.** On Nov 1, 2026, review traced every line of this page to the source of truth and flagged a promise the product didn't support yet. It was cut the same day. The rule: no claim goes public without a source.

## Decisions

**Supplemental practice, not a curriculum.** It adds practice on top of what a family already uses, so it never competes with school.

**Coming soon stays coming soon.** That is the written rule for all site copy, so unbuilt features are labeled that way.

**Spanish waits until it's ready.** The live site lists Spanish as coming soon instead of launching it early.
